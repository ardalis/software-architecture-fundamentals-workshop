CAP, PACELC, and Microservices

The CAP theorem says that when a network partition stops two services from talking, a system must choose between consistency and availability. PACELC adds the rest of the story: if there is a Partition, choose Availability or Consistency; Else, choose lower Latency or Consistency.

Start with the two theorems on their own, using two replicas of one value: CAP (a partition forces the choice) and PACELC (with no partition, waiting for replicas trades latency for consistency).

The other diagrams animate the scenarios from CAP Theorem, PACELC, and Microservices. A client calls Service A, which depends on Services B and C. Each diagram plays the happy path first, then cuts the network and plays it again.
