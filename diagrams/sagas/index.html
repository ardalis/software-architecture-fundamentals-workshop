Saga Patterns

A saga coordinates a workflow that spans several services, each with its own database. Software Architecture: The Hard Parts (Ford, Richards, Sadalage, Dehghani) names eight sagas, one for each combination of three choices: communication (synchronous or asynchronous), consistency (atomic or eventual), and coordination (orchestrated or choreographed).

Every diagram tells the same TicketTide story: a fan buys tickets, so Inventory holds seats, Payments charges the card, and Ticketing issues the tickets. Each diagram plays that happy path first, then resets and replays the purchase with Ticketing failing.
